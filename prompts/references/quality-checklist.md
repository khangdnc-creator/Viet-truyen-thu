# Final quality checklist

Use internally; do not print unless asked.

## Fidelity and canon
- [ ] Essential events and intended outcome are preserved.
- [ ] No major death, betrayal, reveal, relationship change, or battle result was silently invented.
- [ ] Draft wording was distinguished from required content.
- [ ] Exact-text constraints were preserved.

## Dialogue
- [ ] Ordinary draft dialogue has been substantively re-authored where appropriate, not merely synonym-swapped.
- [ ] Facts, intentions, decisions, and emotional turns remain intact.
- [ ] Speakers sound distinct and respond to one another.
- [ ] Subtext supports rather than obscures essential information.
- [ ] No unauthorized revelations, promises, threats, or commitments were added.

## Combat
- [ ] Locations, distances, weapon states, and movements remain plausible.
- [ ] Major actions change position, initiative, risk, or options.
- [ ] Injuries, fatigue, terrain, armor, and numbers matter where relevant.
- [ ] Major outcomes have identifiable causes.
- [ ] Skill matches established ability; no protagonist plot armor by default.
- [ ] The scene avoids repetitive exchanges that change nothing.

## Causality and character
- [ ] Actions arise from understandable motives or pressure.
- [ ] Character knowledge is plausible.
- [ ] Emotional and tactical turns are earned.
- [ ] The chapter has a clear dramatic or thematic purpose.

## Culture and institutions
- [ ] The setting uses institutions appropriate to its period or explicitly invented rules.
- [ ] Chinese imperial bureaucracy, xianxia sects/clans, and Western lordly systems are not conflated without a reason.
- [ ] Family, nation, sect, and species are not reduced to one trait or one agenda.
- [ ] Characters have a plausible reason to be present; no token additions or blanket demographic exclusions.
- [ ] Social rules have practical consequences and are shown through action where possible.

## Prose and delivery
- [ ] Pacing gives important moments enough space without padding.
- [ ] Description is concrete and purposeful.
- [ ] Repetitive phrasing and emotional cues have been reduced.
- [ ] The requested artifact is delivered, not unsolicited process commentary.
- [ ] Only material assumptions or unresolved conflicts are noted.

Fix clear errors before delivery. If a material uncertainty cannot be resolved safely, preserve ambiguity and flag it.
